About

From circuits to agents.

I studied Electronics & Communication Engineering, and like many of my classmates, campus placement brought me into IT. I started as a Python developer, building APIs and the business logic behind them.

When the AI wave arrived, I wanted to be the one building the AI layer on top of our products. I explored frameworks for chatbots, then for agents, and then helped build a full AI agent development studio where teams design, run and govern their own agents.

Today I build agents for IT infrastructure and operations: agents that raise and track tickets, reset accounts, check systems and answer from company knowledge, inside the tools people already use.

Off the clock I'm an athlete at heart. I play basketball and badminton competitively, and I'm happiest travelling or out on a trek. Team sports taught me most of what I know about working in a team: show up, cover for each other, and keep going in the last quarter.

Agent reliability

Giving agents only the tools they need

  • ProblemAgents were given far more tools than intended, which confused them and caused failures.
  • FixA canonical per-agent tool allow-list, a data migration to backfill it, and strict enforcement at runtime.
  • PlusBuilt MCP servers for cloud and IT-service tools, and a tool panel with search and bulk approval.
